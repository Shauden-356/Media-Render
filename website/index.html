Save the media on any page.

Imadia finds the images, audio and video on a web page and saves what you pick: one zip of files, or a video as MP4, MKV, MP3 or M4A. It runs on your own computer or Android phone, and is free.

Pick your system below.

Finds everything

Opens the page the way a browser does, scrolls through it and lists every image, sound and video it loads.

Video and streams

Saves streamed video (HLS, DASH) and the sites yt-dlp supports, with the quality you choose.

On your computer

Nothing goes through our servers: downloads use your own connection, and there is no account.

Stays up to date

Installs new versions by itself, so fixes for changing websites reach you quickly.

All downloads

Linux, ChromeOS, iPhone and iPad are not supported.

Installing

Mac

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Imadia into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software. Close that message, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Android

  1. Open the downloaded .apk from your notifications or the Downloads app.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

Windows

  1. Run Imadia-win-x64.exe.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info › Run anyway.

Imadia isn't on Google Play or the App Store, which don't allow video downloaders. The Mac and Windows warnings appear because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate. The installers are built from the public source code on GitHub.
