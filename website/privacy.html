Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: we do not collect, sell or share personal information. Imadia runs on your own computer or phone and has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

This website

  • It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers, advertising or third-party scripts.
  • To show the latest version and file sizes, your browser asks GitHub's public API (api.github.com) for the latest release. GitHub receives that request, including your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • The download files are served by GitHub, which receives the download request in the same way.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to run and protect the service, under the Vercel Privacy Policy. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.

The Imadia app (Mac, Windows and Android)

  • Nothing is sent to us. The app has no account, analytics or crash reporting, and does not send us information about you or what you download.
  • Websites you use it on. When you give it an address, the app connects to that website directly from your computer, as a browser would. Those websites see the requests as they would from your browser.
  • Updates. The app contacts GitHub to check for and download new versions of itself and of yt-dlp.
  • Page scanner (Mac and Windows). If Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge is not installed, the app downloads a small browser for scanning pages, once, from Microsoft's Playwright download servers.
  • Sign-in cookies (optional). If you choose a browser's sign-ins, a cookies.txt file or pasted cookies, the app keeps them on your device (in storage only you, or on Android only Imadia, can read) and sends each cookie only to the website it belongs to. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Android. Downloads are saved to Downloads › Imadia on your device. Besides internet access, it only asks to show notifications, so downloads can keep running in the background. It does not use contacts, location, the camera, the microphone or access to your other files.
  • Your files. Downloads are saved where you choose. Temporary files are kept on your computer and deleted automatically.
  • The built-in server that powers the app's window only accepts connections from your own computer (127.0.0.1), and only from the app itself.

Children

Imadia is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
