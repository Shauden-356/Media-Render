Installing

Windows

  1. Open the downloaded .exe.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer, then open Media Zipper from the Start menu.

Mac

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Media Zipper into Applications.
  2. Open it. When macOS says it can't verify the app, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway.

The warning appears because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate. You only see it the first time.

What it does

  • Scans any pageLists every image, audio file and video, including ones loaded by scripts.
  • Zips what you pickSelect files and save them as one zip.
  • Saves videosStreams (.m3u8), direct files and YouTube, as MP4 or MKV.
  • Nothing else to installFFmpeg and yt-dlp are built in and kept up to date.
